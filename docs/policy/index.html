Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Gramtask. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. We keep it simple: we only collect what is necessary to run our service, and we don't sell your data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of information needed to provide our services:

  • Account Information: If you create an account, we collect your email address and basic profile information.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous data about how you interact with our website to improve our performance and user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our service.
  • To improve, personalize, and expand our platform based on your feedback.
  • To communicate with you regarding updates, support, or essential service announcements.

4. Data Sharing

We are transparent about who sees your data. We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers strictly necessary to run this platform.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable and standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete the information we have on you. If you wish to delete your account or request data removal, please contact us.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.

Email: contact@gramtask.com
